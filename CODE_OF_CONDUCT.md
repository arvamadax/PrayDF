# Code of Conduct

PrayDF follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, we want PrayDF to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@arvamadax](https://github.com/arvamadax). For a private report, follow the contact steps in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues, and may ban contributors who do not follow this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
